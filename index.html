Portfolio note: for the Ko-fi team

Writing for people who make things, and the people who support them

A short selection of work that maps onto what Community Lead actually needs: voice that sounds like a person, not a brand, and writing that moves between formats without losing its footing.

I pulled these three pieces specifically because they mirror what you're hiring for: writing for independent creators, adapting voice across a huge range of readers, and giving a fast-moving, resource-limited team a brand voice that holds together. Full portfolio is linked at the bottom if you want more.

Silicon Valley · Short-form creator content

Scripts for a platform built to counter scroll-fatigue
T-Labs: video scripts, polls, mindfulness prompts

I wrote short-form scripts for a platform designed to outperform typical Instagram and TikTok content on watch-through, while doing something a bit more ambitious: rebuilding trust between viewers and what they watch. The brief was blunt. Every script had to land with a grandmother in Manila, a factory worker in Brazil, and a CEO in Amsterdam, without changing what it was trying to say for any of them.

The job wasn't writing for an audience. It was writing for many audiences at once, in the same sixty seconds.

Monavida · Creator pipeline & DM voice

Managing and writing for a UGC creator community
Dutch lingerie brand: creator outreach, DM templates, whitelisting strategy

I managed the creator pipeline for a body-positive lingerie brand, writing outreach and DM templates specifically for postpartum and curvy creators. This wasn't generic influencer copy. It had to read as genuinely warm and specific to each creator's situation, while still being efficient enough to send at scale. I also built out the whitelisting/Partnership Ads approach as the brand's key differentiator against competitors doing standard affiliate deals.

Creators can tell the difference between a template and someone who actually looked at their page first. The writing has to earn that.

SoulWork Company · Full brand voice, one person

Homepage rewrite and tone-of-voice build from a cold email
Coaching brand: website copy, positioning, ongoing voice work

This client came from a cold email, not a brief handed to me. I had to work out the brand's voice myself, then build the homepage around it, then keep that voice consistent as the relationship continued. It's the closest thing in my recent work to owning a brand's tone from end to end, which is close to what this role asks someone to do for Ko-fi.
